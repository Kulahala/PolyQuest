# PolyQuest Roadmap

## Purpose And Scope

PolyQuest is a UE 5.8 C++ GAS-first, single-player stylized action RPG. It rebuilds the proven player-facing contracts from the UE 5.7 `Test` project, rather than performing an in-place copy of its FSM implementation, assets, or save data.

The previous `Test` project remains the FSM behavior reference and validation baseline. PolyQuest reuses proven gameplay contracts after deliberate GAS redesign, but does not retain its C++ state machine, save schema, authored assets, or Marketplace content wholesale.

### Migration Rules

- Preserve the player-visible contract when it is validated; redesign its runtime ownership for GAS rather than reproducing old fields, booleans, `EActionState`, or montage-delegate code.
- Animation is the timing and presentation source. Notify and NotifyState implementations send narrow semantic events to the active ability; only abilities, effects, and the hit resolver mutate gameplay.
- A completed, cancelled, or interrupted Montage callback from a replaced combo entry must never end or clear the successor entry. The GAS combo stage owns an explicit stale-callback guard and one `EndAbility` cleanup route.
- DataAssets hold authored references and tuning, never active input buffers, current combo indexes, mutable targets, or save state.
- Motion Warping remains conditional on a selected product animation set having valid root-motion alignment needs. It is not a foundation prerequisite and is not enabled merely because Test used it.
- Do not migrate a behavior merely because Test contained it. Each stage must retain its explicit player benefit, prerequisites, and fresh PolyQuest validation fixture.

## Accepted Technical Direction

- **Test migration boundary:** migrate verified player-facing behavior and acceptance cases, not the old FSM, `EActionState`, save identifiers, or authored asset topology.
- **Gameplay authority:** GAS owns ability activation, costs, blocking/cancellation tags, combat state, damage, poise, hit reaction, and death. Animation owns timing and presentation; it is not a second gameplay state source.
- **Input intent:** Physical controls express stable player intent; the active weapon/loadout and GAS determine the concrete ability. Mapping Contexts change for control modes, not for profession labels or weapon inventory alone.
- **Camera and facing:** PolyQuest uses an elevated oblique-perspective follow camera, not a Souls-style over-the-shoulder orbit. Movement remains camera-relative. Unlocked ordinary locomotion faces movement; a valid local screen-space lock continuously faces eligible grounded locomotion/Guard/non-Root-Motion Parry at `800 degrees/second`, while Sprint preserves the lock but restores free movement-facing. Active attack, Dodge, hit reaction, Bow, Root Motion, death, and Stunned retain yaw ownership. Confirmed locked-target death alone hands off once clockwise; all other invalidation clears. Lock-on adds no camera orbit, camera yaw ownership, free look, or global projectile-targeting role; Bow and future projectile spells retain the weapon-family target-assist contract in `TODO-03B`.
- **Data ownership:** a PolyQuest character/ability manifest composes focused authored assets. Combo entries, action settings, enemy attack profiles, and reaction data are modular tuning inputs; none stores mutable gameplay state or replaces ability/effect ownership.
- **Authored tuning source of truth:** balance values such as an authored MoveSpeed GameplayEffect multiplier live in the current Editor asset and are validated by Editor readback/PIE, not duplicated as durable README, architecture, or Roadmap constants. Native no-Content fixtures may use controlled numbers to prove lifecycle and synchronization, but their numbers do not declare production tuning.
- **Weapon presentation axes (completed `TODO-03A6C`):** MainHand base locomotion and durable OffHand presentation are separate facts. A Shield-equipped state derives only from the current committed OffHand definition's `bProvidesShieldPresentation`; active Shield Guard additionally requires exact `State.Action.Guarding.Shield`. Neither `Ability.Defense.Guard.Shield` nor generic `State.Action.Guarding` is a valid Shield-equipped signal. Bow retains its independent aim-state route. This does not redesign equipment transactions or authorize unbounded weapon-pair BlendSpace variants.
- **Equipment evolution:** `TODO-03A` remains a completed player-only single-StaticMesh melee foundation, not a generic weapon framework. The accepted expansion starts with an immutable `UWeaponDefinition` base while retaining `UMeleeWeaponDefinition` as the compatible melee subtype, then uses per-weapon definitions such as `DA_Weapon_Sword`, optional OffHand Shield definitions, TwoHanded MainHand definitions such as Bow/Staff/Greatsword, direct per-weapon ability-class grants, and Enemy Attack Sets; shared Sword/Katana behavior is expressed by shared ability and combo assets, not a mandatory Player Combat Style DataAsset. MainHand/OffHand composition with TwoHanded exclusivity, full Unarmed fallback, direct drop-swap, Shield defense overrides, ordinary weighted enemy attacks, static-enemy shared socket geometry, and the bounded defense-outcome extension are dependency-ordered `TODO-03A1` through `TODO-03A5B`, then `TODO-03AI` -> `TODO-03AI1` -> `TODO-03H1`, plus `TODO-03A4B` when partial Guard is first required (`TODO-03A1` has delivered the domain contract). A Blueprint pickup/preset is presentation and level authoring, not an alternate combat authority or collision-damage path: a generic world-pickup Blueprint may reference an authored weapon definition with optional `BP_Pickup_Sword`/`BP_Pickup_Axe` preset children, `BP_Player_Sword`/`BP_Enemy_Sword` gameplay classes are forbidden, and any authoring preview that visualizes markers/sweep may not use a static collision box or overlap events as melee damage truth.
- **Mode-specific quick slots:** physical `1-4` intents are stable, but their semantic selection is equipment-specific: prepared compatible ability classes registered by one-handed MainHand/OffHand equipment in close combat, and compatible projectile definitions when a TwoHanded Bow is the active MainHand. The Bow directly uses LMB draw/charge/fire plus its authored RMB Guard/Q Parry fallback; the current Guard is full absorption, while an intentional partial-block weapon is gated by `TODO-03A4B`. Rest-site preparation and persistence belongs to `TODO-03D1`; no equipment item or Blueprint grants an implicit permanent physical-slot ownership.
- **Top-down ranged/magic targeting:** Bow and future projectile spells use action-facing for launch presentation, not free vertical mouse aim. The active Ability may acquire one valid hostile target and pass a 3D target snapshot to the spawned projectile; that projectile owns homing after launch and its invalid/lost-target fallback. Held Bow/Staff meshes remain equipped display components, while only independently travelling arrows, bolts, or persistent area effects become Actors. Target selection, visibility/elevation policy, and homing limits are weapon-family stage contracts, not persistent state on a WeaponDefinition or a general lock-on prerequisite.
- **Projectile reuse boundary:** Player Bow and future ranged enemies share the narrow projectile runtime/data and projectile-specific GAS delivery contract, not their input, target acquisition, AI, or equipment ownership. Avoid an abstract player/enemy projectile inheritance tree until a second projectile lifecycle demonstrates a real subtype boundary; arrows/bolts may share the concrete travelling-projectile path, while beams and persistent area effects remain separate actors/effects.
- **Combo contract:** one active light-attack ability owns at most one buffered LMB continuation. `ComboWindow` accepts it, `ComboBranchWindow` consumes early input or retries late input, and `CancelWindow` exposes only a scoped immediate-cancel opportunity for preflight-valid actions.
- **Montage lifetime:** a successor combo entry must explicitly reject stale completion/interruption events from a prior entry. Natural completion, cancellation, hit/death teardown, invalid timing events, and asset failure converge through one ability cleanup path.
- **Combat timing:** weapon collision, potion heal, projectile release, parry active frames, hyper armor, combo windows, and cancellation windows are semantic animation events. Their receiver validates the active ability and current target/context before applying gameplay. Light, Charged, and Sprint Attack use shared `AttackTraceWindow` Begin/End events; the retired action-specific one-shot hit events are not a second damage path.
- **Enemy AI:** StateTree is the default high-level behavior brain for both ordinary enemies and Bosses. It owns patrol, alert, chase, combat intent, return-home, phase selection, and high-level transitions; it requests GAS abilities rather than directly applying combat mutations.
- **Bosses:** Bosses use a separate StateTree and combat data profile, not a different AI framework merely because they do not patrol. Encounter, fog-gate, persistence, and rewards remain outside the Boss StateTree.
- **No parallel FSM:** do not retain an enemy HFSM or `EEnemyState` as a competing runtime truth beside StateTree and GAS tags.
- **Behavior Tree escalation gate:** do not introduce Behavior Trees unless concrete production behavior requires deeply nested reactive arbitration, dynamic subtrees, or broad concurrent services that remain unclear and untestable in StateTree.
- **External resources:** `Content/Assets/` is a raw source reservoir. A resource becomes an imported product integration only when an approved stage names it and validates its exact Skeleton, socket, animation, or gameplay surface.

## Recommendations

- **Behavior Trees:** keep Behavior Tree tooling available but unadopted. Re-evaluate it only at the documented escalation gate; do not split ordinary enemies and Bosses across AI frameworks preemptively.
- **Motion Warping:** add product-level Motion Warping only when the selected stylized animation set contains a concrete root-motion alignment requirement.
- **Shared Dodge/Sprint key:** `TODO-01C2` establishes one temporal physical-input route: a short release requests Dodge and a `0.15s` hold resolves to Sprint intent. Do not rebind independent legacy `IA_Dodge` and `IA_Sprint` actions to the same key, because their independent Started callbacks would race rather than share the arbiter's release/cancel semantics.
- **Future backpack interaction:** only after a backpack has an accepted ownership, capacity, transaction, and SaveGame contract may a short `E` place a compatible pickup into that backpack while a held `E` preserves the direct-equipment/drop-swap behavior from `TODO-03A3`. It must use one shared input arbiter rather than independent short- and long-press bindings, and is not part of the no-inventory 03A3 implementation.
- **Conditional Enemy Melee Trace lifecycle adoption:** `UMeleeTraceSourceComponent` and `UAbilityTask_MeleeTraceWindow` are already shared; only `UEnemyMeleeAbility`'s local open/close wrapper remains outside `FMeleeTraceWindowLifecycle`. Do not migrate it merely because an additional Enemy weapon DataAsset exists. Re-evaluate when a second Enemy melee Ability/archetype repeats that wrapper, or when a planned Enemy-combat change must touch it, and only after a real Enemy ASC/AI fixture proves AttackProfile damage/Guard-Stamina snapshotting, ordinary open/close, closed-task replacement, synchronous endpoint fail-closed behavior, stale Notify isolation, and terminal Trace/Trail cleanup. Reuse the existing scalar helper then; do not create an Enemy-only helper or change AI/AttackProfile ownership.
- **Conditional Launch-facing smoothing:** C3I intentionally uses zero-duration `SetActorRotation` at Commit, so neither `CharacterMovement::RotationRate` nor the Enemy post-Root-Motion recovery rate can soften that snap. Adopt a bounded Player/Enemy Launch-facing refinement only when slowed visual review or playtesting proves the snap is materially stiff: freeze start yaw, target yaw, and launch velocity at reaction receipt; let the active Launch Ability own a short pre-launch turn at a finite rate; then reach the exact target yaw and call the existing `LaunchCharacter` once. Preserve Player hit-reaction yaw suppression, the exact active Enemy Launch-Spec Controller gate, Root-Motion priority, and all cancellation cleanup. Do not interpolate after airborne travel begins, recalculate velocity after turning, or use generic CharacterMovement/Controller yaw or Motion Warping without a separately accepted stage.
